Percents

Percent means “per hundred.” Percents are numbers with the symbol %.

Percent means parts out of 100

15% = 15100 = 0.15

15 shaded squares out of 100 squares means 15 percent.

Common percents

50% = 50100 = 0.50 = 12
25% = 25100 = 0.25 = 14
75% = 75100 = 0.75 = 34

Changing decimals to percents

Rule: Move the decimal point two places to the right and put the % sign next to it.

0.25 → 25%

Changing fractions to percents

Rule: Divide the fraction to get a decimal, then change the decimal to a percent.

25 = 0.40 = 40100 = 40%
