Adding by breaking numbers apart

Often we can add and subtract small enough numbers in our head without needing a calculator or paper.

One useful trick is to break numbers apart into tens and ones.

36 + 23

36 + 20 + 3
= 56 + 3
= 59

Subtracting by using nearby numbers

Subtracting 19 can be easier if we subtract 20 and then add 1 back.

42 − 19

42 − 20 + 1
= 22 + 1
= 23

Another example

59 + 23

59 + 20 + 3
= 79 + 3
= 82
60 − 1 + 20 + 3
= 80 + 2
= 82


Rule: Numbers like 10, 20, 30 and so on are easier to calculate with. Rewrite the addition or subtraction using these numbers, then add or subtract the smaller remaining part.

Notes to Parents



Notes to Parents:
Mental arithmetic helps a young learner to acquire "mental placement" of numbers and leads to greater numeracy and mathematucak reasoning. Reward correct answers with a jellybean and have fun playing such games with them.
