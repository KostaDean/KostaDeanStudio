Equal Fractions

Equal fractions have the same value, even when they use different numbers.

Examples

23 = 46 = 2 × 23 × 2
12 = 36 = 1 × 32 × 3

Rule: Multiplying the numerator and the denominator by the same number keeps the value of a fraction the same.
