Mental Add/Subtract with Easy Numbers

Use easy numbers like 10, 20, 30, 100, and 200 to add and subtract quickly.

Easy numbers

Easy numbers are numbers like 10, 20, 30, 200, and so on.

Mental add/subtract is especially easy with such numbers.

46 + 20 = 66
27 + 30 = 57
218 + 10 = 228
218 + 200 = 418

The rule



Rule: Special number add/subtract is adding or subtracting on the same column of place value. We keep the same digits in the other columns and change only the matching place.

Examples

Example 1: 46 + 20

46 + 20 = 66

Example 2: 218 + 10

218 + 10 = 228

Example 3: 218 + 200

218 + 200 = 418
