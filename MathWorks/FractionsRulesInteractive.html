Fractions Rules

Letters a, b, c, d stand for numbers. This page summarizes the main fraction rules.

1) Fractions mean division

ab means a ÷ b

b indicates the number of parts. a indicates how many parts are taken.

2 parts taken out of 6 parts divided

2) Equal fractions

ab = a × cb × c

Multiplying the numerator and denominator by the same number keeps the value the same.

3) Addition with the same denominator

ac + bc = a + bc

4) Multiplication of fractions

ab × cd = a × cb × d

5) Fractions with different denominators

ab + cd = a × db × d + b × cb × d

6) Division is inverse multiplication

To divide by n is the same as multiplying by 1n.

ab = a × 1b
a = a1
ab = a1 × 1b
