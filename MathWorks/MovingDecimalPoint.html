Moving the Decimal Point

Numbers are written using the decimal system based on ten. Numbers like 1, 10, 100, 1000 are special and very easy to use.

1) Multiplying by 10, 100, 1000...

Multiplying by a special number moves the decimal point to the right as many places as there are zeros.

6.738
→
67.38
→
673.8
100 × 6.738 = 673.8

100 has two zeros, so the decimal point moves two places to the right.

2) Dividing by 10, 100, 1000...

Dividing by a special number moves the decimal point to the left as many places as there are zeros.

251.04
→
25.104
251.04 ÷ 10 = 25.104

10 has one zero, so the decimal point moves one place to the left.

Writing decimals as fractions

Clear the decimal point and divide by 1 with as many zeros as places the decimal point moved.
