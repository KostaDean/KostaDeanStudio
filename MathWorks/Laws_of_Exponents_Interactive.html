Laws of Exponents

Learn the laws of exponents from repeated multiplication.

Definition of Exponents

an=a·a·a...a
24=2·2·2·2=16

Law 1

an·am=an+m
x2·x3=x5

Law 2

an/am=an-m
x7/x2=x5

Law 3

(an)m=anm
(x2)4=x8

Law 4

a0=1

Law 5

a-1=1/a

Law 6

a1/n=n√a
The nth root of a is the number multiplied by itself n times to get a.
