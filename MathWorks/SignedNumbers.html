Signed Numbers

On the number line, moving to the right is positive (+). Moving to the left is negative (−).

Positive and negative numbers

A number to the right of 0 is a positive number. A number to the left of 0 is a negative number.

→

We use parentheses to include the sign of the number: (−5), (−1), (+3), (+6).

Greater and less

Rule: On the number line, if a number is to the right of another number it is greater (>); if it is to the left it is lesser (<).

(−10) < (+2)
(−1) > (−100)

Signed numbers as arrows

A signed number can be shown as an arrow. The sign tells the direction. The number tells the magnitude, or how long the arrow is.

(+3)

An arrow 3 units to the right

(−5)

An arrow 5 units to the left

The dark mark shows where the vector begins. The small arrow shows direction. The last mark shows the magnitude.
