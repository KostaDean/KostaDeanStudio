From Words to Math

Certain words and phrases express math operations. These are the keys to solving word problems.

Words that become math

In word problems, words tell us which operation to use.

of often means multiplication
Example: 1/2 of 10 means (1/2) × 10.
is often means equals
Example: 1/2 of 10 is 5 means (1/2)×10 = 5.
what often means the unknown x
Example: What is 25% of 20?
per often means division
Example: $15 per dozen means 15/12 dollars each.
times means multiplication
Example: 3 times the price means 3p.
percent means per hundred
Example: 25% means 25/100.

Example 1: of and is

The sentence “1/2 of 10 is 5” becomes:

12 × 10 = 5

of became multiplication. is became equals.

Example 2: what means unknown

The sentence “What is 25% of 20?” becomes:
