Along the Number Line

Use steps from the origin to locate where you are on a number line.

The idea

Consider a number line with each number notched on it being that number of "steps" from the origin at 0.

Where are you if you start at 0 and move along the number line? Moving to the right means adding. Moving to the left means subtracting.

How to read movement

Example 1: Start at 5 and go 6 steps to the right.

5 + 6 = 11

Example 2: Start at 12 and go 3 steps to the left, then 5 steps to the right.

12 − 3 + 5 = 14

Example 3: Start at −2 and go 6 steps to the right.

−2 + 6 = 4



About Math



About Math:
The Numberline is a very important idea in Math. It associates numbers with points on the Numberline. It also provides a mental picture that can more easily and simply model the operations of addition and subtraction. And later of "signed numbers".

Notes to Parents



Notes to Parents:
Having "mental pictures" that describe Mathematical ideas is very important in understanding and doing Math. The Numberline is one such mental picture. These exercises aim at such mental picture development of Math for young students.

Exercises

Type the number where you land.

1) Start at 5 and go 6 steps to the right. Where are you?
2) Start at 12 and go 3 steps to the left, then 5 steps to the right. Where are you?
3) Start at −2 and go 6 steps to the right. Where are you?
4) Start at −3 and go 2 steps to the left. Where are you?
5) Start at 2 and go 3 steps to the left, then 4 steps to the right. Where are you?
6) Start at 20 and go 10 steps to the left, then 5 steps to the right. Where are you?
7) Start at 20 and go 10 steps to the right, then go 10 more steps to the right. Where are you?
8) Start at 20 and go 20 steps to the right, then 10 steps to the left. Where are you?
