Ratios

Learn how ratios compare quantities and connect to fractions.

What is a ratio?

A ratio compares two quantities.

For example, if we have 2 apples and 1 orange, we compare apples to oranges.

🍎 🍎   :   🍊
Apples : Oranges = 2 : 1

We read 2 : 1 as “two to one,” or 2 apples for every 1 orange.

Ratio language

Ratios can be expressed in words. These all describe the same ratio:

5 to 2
5 per 2
5 for every 2
5 : 2

The word per often means a ratio. Miles per hour, dollars per pound, and feet per second are all ratios.

A ratio is a fraction

A ratio can also be written as a fraction.

5 : 2 = 5 / 2

The ratio 5 : 2 has value 5 divided by 2.



Parent Note: It is helpful to connect ratios with fractions. A ratio is another way of writing a fraction that compares two quantities.

Preparing for Algebra

If we name one quantity A and the second quantity B, then their ratio A to B is the fraction:

A : B = A / B

This language also prepares students for Algebra, where letters are used to name quantities.

If A = 6 and B = 3,
A : B = 6 : 3 = 6 / 3 = 2

Easy numbers first

Use easy numbers to understand the idea. Use a calculator when the numbers become harder.

4 : 2 = 4 / 2 = 2
10 : 5 = 10 / 5 = 2

These two ratios have the same value. They are called equivalent ratios. 

The rule



Rule: A ratio compares two quantities. A ratio can be written with words, with a colon, or as a fraction. If the quantities are A and B, then the ratio A to B is A / B.

Exercises

Type ratios with a colon, like 5:2. Equivalent answers such as 10:5, 2:1, 10/5, or 2 are accepted when they mean the same value.

1) Write “3 to 1” as a ratio.

2) Write “5 per 2” as a ratio.

3) 4 cats and 2 dogs. What is the ratio of cats to dogs?

4) 6 red blocks and 3 blue blocks. What is the ratio of red to blue?

5) Write 8 : 4 as a fraction.

6) Find the value of 8 : 4.

7) If A = 10 and B = 5, find A : B.

8) If A = 10 and B = 5, find A / B.

9) Find the value of 15 : 5.

10) Write “7 for every 2” as a ratio.
