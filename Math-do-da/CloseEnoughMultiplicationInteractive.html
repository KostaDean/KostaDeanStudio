Close Enough Multiplication

Use nearby easy numbers to estimate multiplication quickly.

Why close enough multiplication helps

Long-hand multiplication on paper can sometimes be hard.

When an exact answer is not needed, it is best to use a close enough number instead.

A close enough result is all that is needed to estimate, check, or understand the size of an answer.

Use special numbers

Special numbers like 10, 100, and 1000 make mental multiplication easier.

We replace a hard number with a nearby easy number, then multiply.

The approximately equal symbol

The symbol ≈ means approximately equal to, or close to, but not exactly equal to.

394 × 287 ≈ 120,000

This means 120,000 is close to the exact answer.

Examples

Example 1: Estimate 394 × 287.

394 × 287 ≈ 400 × 300 = 120,000

Example 2: Division can also use close enough numbers.

217 ÷ 53 ≈ 200 ÷ 50 = 4

The rule



Rule: For close enough multiplication, replace hard numbers with nearby easy numbers. Then multiply the easy numbers.
