Coordinates and Graphs

Each point on the number line can be identified by a number.

Points on a number line

A number line names each point by one number.

Here the purple point is at 2.

The coordinate plane

Similarly, we can identify each point on the coordinate plane by two numbers called coordinates.

The numbers in parentheses, such as (3, 2), are called the coordinates of the point.

The first number is the x-coordinate, measured along the horizontal x-axis.

The second number is the y-coordinate, measured along the vertical y-axis.

Quadrants

The x-axis and y-axis divide the coordinate plane into four parts called quadrants.

Example: The point P(3, 5) is in QI.

The point P(−3, −2) is in QIII.

If the x-coordinate is negative and the y-coordinate is positive, the point is in QII.
