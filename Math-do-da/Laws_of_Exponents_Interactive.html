Laws of Exponents

Learn the laws of exponents from repeated multiplication.

Definition of Exponents

an=a·a·a...a
24=2·2·2·2=16

Law 1

an·am=an+m
x2·x3=x5

Law 2

an/am=an-m
x7/x2=x5

Law 3

(an)m=anm
(x2)4=x8

Law 4

a0=1

Law 5

a-1=1/a

Law 6

a1/n=n√a
The nth root of a is the number multiplied by itself n times to get a.

About Math



About Math:
All theorems and laws in Math ultimately derive from mathematical definitions and self-evident axioms/postulates taken to be true. Using strict logical reasoning we first prove these theorems/laws before they are then used as being true.

The Laws of Exponents are good examples of this deductive process of math. As all these Laws can be directly derived from the very definition of exponents and laws of numbers.

Notes to Parents



Notes to Parents:
Encourage your young learner to understand and make sense of the rules they use to do math. The Laws of Exponents is an ideal place for that practice. As each of these can quickly and easily be verified by simple examples that make sense.
Knowledge based on understanding and not memory is far more lasting and fun to use.
