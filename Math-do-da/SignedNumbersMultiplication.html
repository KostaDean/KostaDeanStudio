Signed Numbers Multiplication

Important: The negative of a number on the number line is that number in the opposite direction.

negative means “opposite”

The negative of (+4) is (−4). The negative of (−5) is (+5).

Multiplying by −1

To multiply by (−1) is to take the opposite of the number.

(−1) × (+4) = (−4)
(−1) × (−5) = −(−5) = (+5)

Often we do not write the multiplication sign ×, but write the numbers next to each other.

(−2)(+4) means (−2) × (+4)

Multiplying two signed numbers

Rule: Multiplying two numbers with the same sign is positive. Multiplying two numbers with different signs is negative.

SignsResult signExample
(+)(+)positive(+2)(+4) = (+8)
(−)(−)positive(−2)(−4) = (+8)
(−)(+)negative(−2)(+4) = (−8)
(+)(−)negative(+2)(−4) = (−8)

Notes to Parents



Notes to Parents:
The best way to think and work and make sense of signed numbers is to imagine them on the number line. Have your young learner use the number line to visualize signed numbers. Especially visualizing the "opposite" (negative) of the number. And knowing multiplication by -1 is just taking the "opposite" of the number. Then the rules for multiplying signed numbers will make more sense to them.
