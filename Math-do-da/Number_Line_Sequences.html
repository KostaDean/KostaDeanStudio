Notes to Parents



Notes to Parents:
Visualizing numbers along the number line is very important. Many math ideas are developed using the number line and coordinate plane and higher dimensional spaces. And lays the foundations of connecting Algebra and Geometry. 

Have your young learner locate numbers along the number line by finding what other numbers are less than and greater than the given number.
