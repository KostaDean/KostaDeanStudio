Adding Fractions

Rule: You cannot add fractions unless they have the same denominators.

The denominator shows how many equal parts the whole is subdivided into. The greater the denominator, the more equal parts there are, and the smaller in size each part is.

3 equal parts
2 equal parts

Rule: You can only add fractions with equal size parts. The following fractions cannot directly be added. First we need to equivalently rewrite each of these to have the same dinominator (have equal size subdivisions).

23 + 12

First we need to make the subdivision parts match. That is done by having equivalent fractions with same dinominator.

same whole subdivided into 6 equal parts taking 4 such parts is same as subdivided into 3 equal parts and taking 2 such parts.
same whole subdivided into 6 equal parts taking 3 such parts is the same as subdivided into 2 equal parts and taking 1 such part.
46 + 36 = 76
