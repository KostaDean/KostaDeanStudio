Welcome parents!

Math-do-da! lets you preview every lesson fully before assigning it.

To create a Learning Path:
• Open a lesson from the lesson list.
• Complete all of its exercises yourself.
• At the Winner Window, tap “Select for Learning Path.”
• Return to the lesson list and preview another lesson.
• Near the top of the lesson page, tap “Name/Save Learning Path” when your selections are complete.

Your young learner can then follow the saved Learning Path on this same phone, tablet, or computer. Completed lessons receive check marks, and the next lesson opens automatically until the path is finished.

Keep sessions friendly, short, and low-pressure. Encourage discovery rather than speed.

These free lessons were first written by a retired mathematics teacher for his two granddaughters.
